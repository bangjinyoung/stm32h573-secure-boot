Firmware: commits 0332fe2 (boot, two images), 73531b8 (cmake, per-target cmse), 0435969 (Secure app),
118d5b3 (Non-secure app), e530643 (scripts). Built at e530643 (BUILD_ID "e530643" in bl2c.bin, s_v3.bin
and ns_v1.bin), Release, STM32CubeCLT 1.22.0 GCC:

    cmake -S . -B build/m10 -G Ninja -DCMAKE_TOOLCHAIN_FILE=cmake/arm-none-eabi.cmake \
          -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DBL2_ROLLBACK=hw
    cmake --build build/m10
    scripts/make_m10_images.sh build/m10

randomized: a rerun gives other sha256 values and is reproduced by the properties below.

Vector tables: s_v3 sp=0x30040000 reset=0x0c018ded (BL2 accepts sp in (0x30000000, 0x300A0000]);
ns_v1 sp=0x200a0000 reset=0x080207ed (tz_s accepts sp in (0x20050000, 0x200A0000], reset in the NS slot).

imgtool runs with --overwrite-only: without it the trailer is sized for swap (0x1850) and the 29K Secure image
does not fit the 0x8000 slot.
