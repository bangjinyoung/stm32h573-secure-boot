Findings:

- The hash failure and the signature failure take different paths on the board: the last
  validation line is EXPECTED_HASH_TLV for CT-APP-HASH and EXPECTED_SIG_TLV plus
  bootutil_verify_sig for CT-APP-SIG (D-08 kept with upstream MCUboot via D-14).
- The HDPL1 -> HDPL2 boundary is unchanged by the bootloader swap: STiRoT rejects a one-bit
  change in the HDPL2 image whether it is OEMuRoT or the custom BL2.
- End state differs: OEMuRoT jumps to the System Bootloader when no image is valid
  (MCUBOOT_EXT_LOADER); the custom BL2 stays in its own loop. Both leave the tampered app unexecuted.
- E8-K: the 1059-line VCP log has no run of 16 or more hex bytes and no 32-hex-digit string.
  BL2 has no image encryption and no key print path.

Observation outside the verdicts (Open state, SW1=0): after each UR write the core ran from
SECBOOTADD without STiRoT. With the tampered BL2 in place (after w7 and w9 writes) it still ran,
printed `hdpl=0x51` (HDPL1) and booted the app through RSS to HDPL3. In Open, BOOT0 lets
unverified code run at HDPL1, above the HDPL2 STiRoT gives the verified BL2. The closed-state
behavior belongs to M11.
