version 2.0.0 as the OEMuRoT image it replaced, and started it at HDPL2 (E8.1; R4 settled).

## Fault (read-only, `2026-10-05-m8-b1-fault.log`)

| register | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| MMFAR | 0x08020000 | secondary slot header, Non-secure alias |
| HFSR | 0x40000000 | FORCED |
| SHCSR | 0x00000004 | |
| MPU_CTRL | 0x00000003 | ENABLE, HFNMIENA; PRIVDEFENA clear |
| MPU_TYPE | 0x00000C00 | 12 regions |

MPU regions 0-7 as STiRoT left them (RBAR / RLAR): 0 0x0FF8A284/0x0FF93943 (system flash),
1 0x0C000004/0x0C017FE5 (HDPL2 code, RO exec), 2 0x0C018001/0x0C1FFFE7 (rest of Secure flash,
RW XN), 3 0x30000001/0x3009FFE5 (SRAM RW XN), 4 0x0FFD0901/0x0FFD1FE7, 5 0x08FFF001/0x08FFF7E7,
6 0x08FFF805/0x08FFFFE7, 7 0x50000001/0x5FFFFFE1 (Secure peripherals). No region covers
Non-secure flash. Reading the regions selected each with a debugger write to MPU_RNR
(0xE000ED98); no other write.

Root cause: STiRoT hands over to HDPL2 with its own MPU set on and no background map. The M2
port assumed the MPU off, so the first Non-secure read of the secondary slot faulted. ST
OEMuRoT clears the MPU first (LL_SECU_DisableCleanMpu) and programs its own regions.

Fix: 4bf2865 (BL2 disables the MPU and clears all regions before the SAU setup; at the handoff
it sets PRIVDEFENA with region 1 disabled). Board left as is: custom BL2 installed and stopped
in MemManage_Handler, ST app in the primary slot, download slot as STiRoT left it.
