from the build in `2026-10-05-m8-h2-h5-host.txt` (BL2 bin and code unchanged since 1a3af09).

The board steps run with `scripts/tamper_step.sh [-n] -u m8 <log dir> <step>` (renamed from
m7_tamper_step.sh; `m7` keeps the M7 table unchanged). r1 and r2 write the M7 OEMuRoT
download image and the ST app back for recovery to the M7 end state.
