Findings
  - SWD Hotplug programming of SECWM-secure flash works only when the halted core is Secure.
    With SW1=1 the System Bootloader runs Non-secure and the flash loader programs through
    FLASH_NSCR, which the secure sectors reject (SECSR PGSERR). Inferred from the registers;
    consistent with M4 S5 (SW1=0, success) and M5 T1 (SW1=1, failure).
  - mode=UR connects failed with SW1=1 (M5 T1b, M6 09:42). Not retried with SW1=0.
