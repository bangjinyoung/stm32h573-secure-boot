# E7.1 CT-BL-DL: tampered encrypted OEMuRoT in the STiRoT download slot, STiRoT refuses it (2026-10-05)

Image a1-oemurot-dl-flip.bin, sha256 fc578366..., rot_enc_sign.bin (OEMuRoT 2.0.0, encrypted) with
ciphertext byte 0x1400 flipped (0x8a -> 0x8b). Same version as the installed OEMuRoT, which
CT-BL-DL-CTRL showed STiRoT accepts, so a refusal here is about the image content, not the counter.
