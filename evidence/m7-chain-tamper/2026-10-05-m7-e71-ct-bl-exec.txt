# E7.1 CT-BL-EXEC: tampered OEMuRoT in its execution area, STiRoT refuses to boot it (2026-10-05)

Image a2-oemurot-exec-flip.bin, sha256 800eb178..., rot_init_sign.bin (OEMuRoT 2.0.0, STiRoT format,
magic 0x73ab1024) with byte 0x1400 flipped (0x92 -> 0x93). The byte sits in USART0_Send at
0x0C001400 (objdump: uxth r2, r2 -> uxth r3, r2). Signature and TLVs untouched.
This is the execution-area tamper deferred in M5 (E5.3), now run on the OEMuRoT image.

M5 criteria (checklist M5): 1 control + 5 tamper in place + 6 restore + (3 PC in bootloader or 4).

## 1. Control

The same chain booted OEMuRoT 2.0.0 and the app at 13:31:27 (W4) and 13:53:29 (W6):
"Starting bootloader OEMuROT 2.0.0" then User App #A (-m7-vcp.log).

  Erasing internal memory sectors [0 11], File download complete, Download verified successfully,
  NSSR 0, SECSR 0, readback 0x0C000000/0x18000 cmp a2: identical (0x1400 = 0x93).
  SW1=0 afterwards: the tampered OEMuRoT ran from SECBOOTADD without STiRoT and stopped at
  "read: Wrong OBK HDPL2 cfg", VTOR 0x0C000400, PC 0x0C00D7A4 (-m7-observe.log 14:02:10).

  3. SWD: VTOR 0x0BF97000 (System Bootloader), DSCSR 0x00020000 (CDS=0, Non-secure), CFSR 0,

## 5. Tamper still in place after the refusal, -m7-w7-postboot-check.log

  Hotplug read of 0x0C000000/0x18000 with the System Bootloader running: identical to a2.
  STiRoT refused the image and left it in place; it did not erase the execution area.

## Reading

- STiRoT checks the OEMuRoT execution area at boot, not only at install: a one-bit change in the
  installed image was enough to stop the chain before OEMuRoT ran.
- CT-BL-EXEC and the CT-APP-* refusals end in the same state (System Bootloader). The OEMuRoT log
  tells them apart: present for CT-APP-*, absent here.
- 6. Restore: W9 at 14:15:28. STiRoT installed the valid OEMuRoT 2.0.0 from its download slot,
  OEMuRoT 2.0.0 and the app booted again, execution area header + body genuine
  (-m7-e71-ct-bl-dl-ctrl.txt).
